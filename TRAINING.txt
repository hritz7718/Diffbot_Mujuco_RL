TARGET-REACHING STARTER

1. Double-click 1 - Setup Training.cmd. It installs Gymnasium and Stable-Baselines3
   into your existing MuJoCo environment, then checks the environment interface,
   reset reproducibility, episode endings and a conventional controller baseline.
   Stop and report any error. Baseline should reach at least 8/10 targets.
2. Double-click 2 - Train DiffBot.cmd. This starts a fresh CPU PPO run of about
   300,000 actions. Training is headless. Duration depends on your computer.
   Each timestamped runs folder has logs, checkpoints, source snapshots and versions.
   Ctrl+C saves an interrupted model; the viewer only selects completed runs.
3. Double-click 3 - Watch DiffBot.cmd after training completes. The green disk is
   the target. Close the viewer to stop. New targets appear after each episode.

TASK: random target 0.4-1.2 m away, random initial yaw; 30 second limit.
Success: chassis centre within 0.12 m of goal. No stopping/docking requirement yet.
Action: normalized forward speed and turning speed, converted to wheel speeds.
Observation: target xy in robot frame, forward speed, yaw rate, wheel speeds.
Reward: distance progress, success bonus, small time/action cost, failure penalty.
Failure: tipping or leaving the 3 m radius training area.
Uses ideal simulator position/velocity: real deployment requires localization.
No obstacles, LiDAR, sensor noise, domain randomization or real robot connection.
Success in this starter does not establish readiness for hardware deployment.
The final printed success rate evaluates 20 seeded targets. Low success means
more investigation/training is needed; completing training is not proof of learning.

This setup has been statically checked by the assistant. Runtime checks and
training must run on your side because its session cannot launch your Python.
